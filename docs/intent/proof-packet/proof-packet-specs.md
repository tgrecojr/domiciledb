# Proof packet — EARS specs

Prefix `PACKET`. Facets: `DATA` (packet assembly and filtering), `PDF` (document content and
rendering), `ROUTE` (the endpoint and the `/report` page).

Markers: `[x]` observed working in current code · `[ ]` specified, not yet implemented or partial
· `[D]` deferred.

## Assembly

- [x] **PACKET-DATA-001**: The proof packet shall include only items whose lifecycle status is `active`, and its item count shall be the number of such items in the requested scope.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-002**: Each packet item's aggregate value shall be computed by the coverage segment's per-item value rule (replacement cost × quantity, a non-finite or negative quantity counting as zero, clamped to the safe-integer range), or no value when the item has no replacement cost.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-003**: An active item with no replacement cost shall be listed in its room but excluded from room, category, and grand totals, and the packet shall report how many items were excluded.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-004**: The proof packet shall group items into rooms by location, placing items with no location or an unknown location in a room named "Unassigned", and shall total each room and the whole packet in integer cents.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-005**: Rooms shall be ordered by name with "Unassigned" always last, and items within a room shall keep the order in which they were read.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-006**: The proof packet shall total valued items by category name, labelling items with no category "Uncategorized", ordered by total descending.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-007**: Room photos shall be attached to the room of their location, and the "Unassigned" room shall never carry room photos.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-008**: The proof packet shall be scoped to the entire household, one location, one category, or one location and one category together, and shall label the scope "Entire household", "Location: <name>", or "Category: <name>".
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-009**: For each item the proof packet shall carry title, manufacturer, model number, serial number, quantity, condition, age estimate, category name, replacement cost, the latest price paid with its purchase date, its photos ordered by sort order then id, and the kind, filename, and stored path of each attached document.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-010**: The proof packet shall carry the household name and address, the generation timestamp, and a coverage summary computed at generation time.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-011**: For the unfiltered household, the proof packet's grand total shall equal the dashboard coverage total for the same items.
- [x] **PACKET-DATA-012**: Room, category, and grand totals shall be accumulated with the coverage segment's clamped addition so that none of them leaves the safe-integer range.

## Document

- [x] **PACKET-PDF-001**: The proof packet PDF shall be Letter size with a running header "<household> — Home Inventory · Proof-of-loss packet" and a footer "Generated by DomicileDB · Informational only — verify with your insurer." with page numbers on every page.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-002**: The first page shall show the household name and address, "Generated <date> · <scope label>", the grand total, the line "<n> valued · <m> not yet valued · <k> active items", the coverage line, a "Totals by category" table when any category has a total, and the informational disclaimer.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-003**: The coverage line shall read "No Coverage B limit on file." when there is no policy, limit, or status, and otherwise "Coverage B limit $X" with a colored pill reading "Within coverage", "Approaching limit", or "Over limit" followed by the rounded percentage used.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-004**: Item pages shall present each room under a header with its name and room total, at most 10 items per page, marking continuation pages "(cont.)"; a room with no active items shall produce no page.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-005**: Up to 4 room photos shall appear on the first page of a room, before its item entries, and on no continuation page.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-006**: Each item entry shall show title and aggregate value (or "No value"), then Category, Manufacturer, Model, Serial, Quantity (only when greater than 1), Condition, Age, "Replacement (each)" (only when quantity is greater than 1 and the item is valued), and "Price paid $X (date)" when known.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-007**: Each item entry shall embed up to 4 of the item's photos, taken from the original files and downscaled to at most 600 px JPEG, loaded one at a time; a photo that cannot be read shall be skipped without failing the packet.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-008**: Each item entry shall list its attached documents as "Attached: <kind> (<filename>), …", one `kind (filename)` entry per document.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-009**: When the scope contains no active items, the proof packet shall render a page reading "No active items to report."
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-010**: Dates in the proof packet shall be formatted en-US with a short month name, and an invalid or missing date shall print as "—".
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-011**: Where an item has attached receipts, the proof packet shall embed up to two receipt images with the item, downscaled like photos, and shall list every attached document by kind and filename; PDF receipts are listed, not rendered.
- [x] **PACKET-PDF-012**: The proof packet PDF shall carry the metadata title "<household> — Home Inventory" and author "DomicileDB".

## Route and page

- [x] **PACKET-ROUTE-001**: The report page shall offer a "Download entire household" link, one link per location when any exist, and one link per category, each pointing at the proof-packet endpoint with the matching scope.
- [x] **PACKET-ROUTE-002**: The proof-packet endpoint shall accept `?location=` and `?category=` only as positive integers, ignoring any other value, and shall respond 404 when no household exists.
- [x] **PACKET-ROUTE-003**: If the scoped item count exceeds `PROOF_PACKET_MAX_ITEMS`, then the proof-packet endpoint shall respond 413 with guidance to narrow by location or category, without rendering.
- [x] **PACKET-ROUTE-005**: While the household's active item count exceeds `PROOF_PACKET_MAX_ITEMS`, the report page shall say the whole-household download is unavailable, state both numbers, and offer only the per-location and per-category packets.
- [x] **PACKET-ROUTE-004**: When the proof-packet endpoint renders, it shall respond with `application/pdf` as an attachment named `home-inventory-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf` (date from the generation timestamp) and `Cache-Control: no-store`.
